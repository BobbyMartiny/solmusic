Privacy Policy for SolMusic

Last updated: October 5, 2026

SolMusic is an Android music player that plays audio files stored on your device. This policy explains how SolMusic accesses and uses information, what it stores, and your choices.

Information accessed and stored on your device

SolMusic accesses local audio files and their media information, such as song titles, artists, albums, artwork, duration, and file locations, to display your library and play your music. It may also read lyrics files you choose to import.

SolMusic stores playlists, saved queues, playback position, listening history and statistics, imported lyrics, and app preferences in its private storage on your device. This information supports features such as resuming playback, showing listening statistics, and remembering your settings.

SolMusic does not transmit this information to the developer or to a SolMusic server. It has no account system, advertising SDKs, remote analytics, third-party tracking, or remote crash-reporting service. SolMusic does not sell personal information.

Device permissions

  • Audio or storage access: Depending on your Android version, SolMusic requests access to audio files or device storage to find, display, and play your local music.
  • Notifications: SolMusic may request permission to show playback notifications and controls.
  • Background playback: SolMusic uses Android's foreground media playback service so music can continue while you use other apps or turn off the screen.

You can manage granted permissions in Android Settings. Revoking audio access may prevent SolMusic from finding or playing your library.

Data sharing and device integrations

SolMusic does not automatically upload your music or app data to external servers. Playback information and controls may be made available through Android's media features, including notifications, widgets, connected playback devices, and Android Auto.

If you choose to share a song, SolMusic provides the selected audio file to the app you select through Android's share menu. That app's handling of the file is covered by its own privacy policy.

Internet access and this webpage

SolMusic's music playback works offline, and the app does not request the Android internet permission. SolMusic does not provide its own cloud backup or synchronization service.

Opening this privacy policy launches your browser and requires an internet connection. This webpage is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub and your browser may process ordinary web request information, such as your IP address, under their own policies. This page contains no advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies added by SolMusic. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Data retention, deletion, and backups

App data is retained in SolMusic's private device storage until you delete it or clear the app's storage. You can delete playlists, saved queues, and imported lyrics, and clear listening history, using the relevant in-app controls.

To remove all locally stored SolMusic app data, use Android Settings > Apps > SolMusic > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall SolMusic. Names of these controls may vary by device. This does not delete the original music files in your shared device storage.

Android or your device manufacturer may back up or transfer app data according to your device settings. Those system services are outside SolMusic's own cloud functionality. Manage any such backups through your device or backup provider; clearing local storage does not necessarily delete an existing system backup.

Data security

SolMusic uses Android's app-private storage for its saved app data. The developer does not receive your music library or listening history. Your device's security and backup settings help protect locally stored data.

Children

SolMusic does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app or send children's information to the developer.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if SolMusic's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised last updated date.

Contact

For questions about SolMusic or this privacy policy, contact the SolMusic developer at bobthemartini@gmail.com.

If you email the developer, your email address and the information you choose to include will be used to respond to your inquiry. Do not include sensitive information or music files unless needed for your request.
